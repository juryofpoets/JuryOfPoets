<div class="stanza lining">"a +1," he says, and declares<br>himself as the vibe police.<br>I wait for the invite that<br>might never actually come,<br>so I invite myself to see <em>The Invite</em> instead.</div>

<div class="stanza">I sit next to the people who know how I like<br>my chai—<em>ek dum kadak</em>—<br>and my man the same way,<br>a quiet heretic revering the empty seat<br>I leave beside me—until he just cannot.</div>

<div class="stanza">The water at my feet along the port<br>touched his coast at Castle Island,<br>a place mystical enough to house a poet's muse.<br>I shall invite myself on that pilgrimage,<br>to stand where my stranger stood too.</div>

<div class="stanza">One day, we shall cross paths along the Charles,<br>as someone else's plus ones.<br>I'd love to see your spontaneity then,<br>pulling me away like a movie cliche<br>with an invite I know will never come—</div>

<div class="stanza lining">to be your permanent +1.</div>
